Virtuosity Drums — Versilian Studios & Karoryfer Samples
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums

Melody Catch v2 conversion: original FLAC -> family-specific high-pass -> stereo 44.1kHz 192kbps MP3.
Kick: kick mic, snare/rim: snare mic, toms: mid mic, cymbals: overhead.
No equal sum of all close mics. Original source files are not re-encoded MP3s.
Two different recordings alternate per layer. Kick/cymbals use genuine recorded round robins.
Snare/rim/toms use neighboring velocity recordings, level-compensated in the engine;
these are NOT described as same-velocity round-robin recordings.
Mid tom remains transposed from low tom. Clap retains the previous body_percussion samples.
